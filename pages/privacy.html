Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026 · BENERAX LLC, Georgia, USA

This Privacy Policy explains what data the BENERAX Shopify App ("the App", "we", "us") accesses, how we use it, how long we keep it, and how it is deleted. It applies to merchants who install the App on their Shopify store.

1. What the App can access

The App requests the minimum access it needs to do its job. With the merchant's authorization, it uses Shopify's product access (write_products) to create and update draft products in the merchant's own store. It also reads limited order and line-item details — order and item identifiers, quantities, prices, and timestamps — to record sales outcomes.

At this stage, the App does not collect your customers' personal data, such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or shipping/billing addresses.

2. What we store

To operate the App, we store:

  • Store identity & connection: your Shopify store domain and the access token needed to draft products on your behalf (stored encrypted at rest).
  • App activity: the trend, preparation, and draft records you create through the App, and technical event logs (e.g. webhook and processing logs) used to run and debug the service.
  • Subscription state: your plan and billing status, synced from Shopify.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. How we use it

We use the data solely to provide the App's functionality: discovering products, preparing them with AI, drafting them into your store, tracking your drafts, and managing your subscription and support.

4. Data retention

Technical activity logs are retained for a limited period (approximately 30 days) and then removed automatically. Store identity, connection, and subscription records are kept while your store has the App installed.

5. Deletion & your rights

When you uninstall the App, we close your store's local state and stop using your access token. In line with Shopify's data-protection requirements, we honor the standard compliance webhooks:

  • shop/redact — when Shopify signals it (typically ~48 hours after uninstall), we delete your store's associated records from our database.
  • customers/redact and customers/data_request — supported for completeness; we hold no customer personal data to return or erase, as we only read limited, non-personal order and line-item details.

You may also request deletion of your data at any time by emailing us.

6. Third parties

The App relies on Shopify (the platform you install it on) and our hosting and AI-processing providers strictly to deliver the service. These providers process data only as needed to operate the App.

7. Security

Access tokens are encrypted at rest, and merchant requests are authenticated. We follow the principle of least access — the App requests write access to products and publications, plus read access to limited order and line-item data used to record sales outcomes, and nothing more.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.

9. Contact

Questions or data requests: support@benerax.com (support) or info@benerax.com (general).
