Technology · BENERAX Core

The AI engine behind everything we build.

Core is the orchestration engine our products run on. It turns raw market signals into prepared, store-ready products — and it does so safely, multi-tenant, and draft-first.

What Core does

Signal in. Store-ready product out.

◔

Trend discovery

Aggregates live market signals to surface products worth selling — before they saturate.

✦

AI enrichment

Prepares each product with AI — SEO titles, descriptions, tags — with a clear raw-vs-prepared view.

◧

Store drafting

Drafts prepared products into the merchant's store. Draft-first: nothing goes live without a human.

⬡

Built to scale safely

Multi-tenant by design — each store's data is isolated, and access is scoped to the minimum required.

One engine, many surfaces

By building products on a shared engine, every improvement to discovery, preparation, or drafting flows to everything we ship.
